# Data Processing Addendum: DRAFT TEMPLATE (not legal advice; have counsel review before use)

Between **[University]** ("Institution") and **MacroNations LLC** ("Provider"). This addendum supplements the service agreement for the Asha AI instructor.

1. **Scope.** Provider processes student and staff personal data and education records only to provide the service, on the Institution's documented instructions.
2. **Ownership.** The Institution owns all course data, student work, grades and records. Provider acquires no rights except to deliver the service.
3. **School official / FERPA.** Provider acts under the Institution's direct control as a school official with a legitimate educational interest and will not redisclose education records except to the subprocessors below.
4. **Prohibited uses.** No sale of data, no advertising or profiling, no use to train or improve AI models, no use outside the service.
5. **Subprocessors.** Supabase (hosting and database); the AI provider selected for the course (default Anthropic); [add]. Provider will give 30 days' notice before adding or replacing one, and the Institution may object.
6. **Security.** Separate database per Institution; encryption in transit and at rest; stored AI keys encrypted; role-based access limited to course staff; audit log of automated actions; daily backups; [add the Institution's required controls].
7. **Retention and deletion.** Retention follows the per-course settings agreed in writing. On termination or request, Provider exports the data to the Institution and deletes it within 60 days, and confirms in writing.
8. **Student rights.** Provider supports access, correction and deletion requests passed on by the Institution, within 30 days.
9. **Incidents.** Provider notifies the Institution without undue delay and within 72 hours of confirming an incident involving personal data, with the facts known and the steps taken.
10. **Automated actions.** Asha acts only within the professor-mode permissions the Institution's staff enable; staff may pause or reverse any action; final grades are held before posting. Human review remains available to every student.
11. **Audit.** On reasonable notice, the Institution may review Provider's security documentation and the audit export for its courses.
12. **Liability and governing law:** [per master agreement].
