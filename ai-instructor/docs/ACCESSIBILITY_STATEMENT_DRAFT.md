# Accessibility statement: DRAFT (an audit is required before this is published)

MacroNations aims to meet WCAG 2.1 level AA for the Asha chat panel and teaching panel. This draft lists what the design provides and what must be audited. It does not claim conformance.

## Provided by design
- Every spoken lecture and reply also appears as text in the chat panel (a transcript is always available); students can use Asha without voice or the avatar.
- Status messages in the chat and review panels are announced to assistive technology (status regions), and controls have text labels.
- Dialogs and the review panel move focus to their heading when opened and return focus when closed.
- Voice and avatar are optional; the live class works with text only.
- Exams: time extensions are supported through per-student exam windows set by the instructor. **verify** the process for accommodations with the university's disability office.

## To audit before publishing
- Full keyboard-only walk-through and screen-reader pass (NVDA/JAWS and VoiceOver) of the chat, review, appeal and teaching panels.
- Colour contrast in light and dark themes; text resize to 200% and reflow at 320 px.
- Captions or transcripts for any recorded video (none are produced by the AI instructor).
- Camera-based proctoring alternatives for students who cannot use a camera.
- Equation and graph descriptions: every graph Asha draws needs a text description.
